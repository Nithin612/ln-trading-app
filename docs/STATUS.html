What the book says

The exit machinery is correct but has nothing to protect. The ₹ profit ladder arms at +₹2,000 and seals peak−₹1,000 above ₹3,000 — yet only 3 of 40 entries ever reached +1R, so most trades faded from a small green back through the original stop. Fixing exits harder cannot fix that; only better entries can.

Open heat is the live risk. It has climbed 20.2% → 49.6% of capital across 24 open positions. The circuit breaker caps daily realised loss and trades/day — it does not cap accumulated open risk. A cross-day portfolio-heat gate is on the watch-list, not built.

The verdict — where the edge is and where it leaks

▲ The edge

  • 80–89 confidence — +0.20R (n=307)
  • Trending ADX ≥ 25 — +0.24R (n=140)
  • Choppy ADX < 20 — +0.12R (n=337)

▼ The leak

  • 70–79 confidence, the bulk — −0.07R (n=434)
  • Transitional ADX 20–25 — −0.10R (n=339)
  • 70–79 × transitional — −0.23R (n=250)

Corpus cohort: Nifty50 daily, 816 decided trades since 2023-09-13, expectancy winsorized at ±10R to kill tiny-SL artifacts. The corpus corrected the noisier live read, which had blamed the choppy regime — choppy is fine at +0.12R; transitional is the real drag.

The nuance that shapes the fix

One cell carries the loss: 70–79 × transitional, n=250 — 31% of the whole corpus, at −0.23R. But 80–89 × transitional is the single best ranked cell at +0.32R (n=75), so a blanket “skip transitional” gate throws away good trades along with the leak. That is exactly what the §8 experiment has to settle before anything ships.

Experiment

Gate experiment — which lever to pull

Read-only backtest over the same corpus, testing the 6.2 verdict before any engine change. The question: raise the confidence gate, skip the transitional regime, or both? Skipping the transitional regime while keeping the 70 gate wins — it nearly doubles captured R and triples per-trade expectancy, and it keeps far more trades than a gate-80 bump.

Its ≈566 above is arithmetic — 816 minus that cell — not a measured result; every other number in this table came out of the script.

Which factors predict edge

Per-factor attribution at corpus scale. Δ is expectancy when the factor supports the trade direction minus expectancy when it is neutral — the direct input for the 6.4 weight retune.

Read these with their samples

MACD_CROSS and RSI_LEVEL are the retune-worthy negatives — n≈130 each, so the signal is real; DARK_CLOUD_COVER and EVENING_STAR are larger deltas on n≈41–49 and deserve a wider corpus before a big weight cut. RSI_DIVERGENCE is not a normal cohort: it hits only 27% of the time yet reaches +1R in 70% of cases, with MFE +3.30R against MAE −2.84R — a wide-stop, wide-swing population. Upweighting it raises variance as well as expectancy.

Decision

What we start next

Ranked by value. Nothing auto-advances — each starts on your command, and everything here is behaviour-changing, so it needs the §8 backtest discipline plus sign-off before it touches scoring or gating.

  1. 1
    Regime-gate §8 experiment → the gate itself RECOMMENDED
    Add the cell-level variant to gate_experiment.py, promote the winner into a proper §8 walk-forward regression, and if it holds, implement the skip as a Market-Context-Engine slice. This is where a measured finding becomes real P&L — the corpus says it roughly doubles captured R. Not a quick one: engine-adjacent, quant-verifier + §8 + your sign-off.
  2. 2
    6.4 — weight retune + shadow→active promotion
    Downweight MACD_CROSS and RSI_LEVEL first (largest samples), treat DARK_CLOUD_COVER and EVENING_STAR as candidates pending more data, and wire the promotion gate the shadow layer feeds. The retune can start now; promotion stays time-gated by forward evidence, which today is 9 resolved shadow signals.
  3. 3
    Cross-day portfolio-heat gate
    Not in any phase plan, and the book argues for it: heat is at 49.6% of capital across 24 open positions while the breaker only caps daily realised loss. An advisory open-risk cap that never weakens the daily-loss breaker. Small, self-contained, and it protects the record that gates go-live.
  4. 4
    Pair-trading / market-neutral candidate
    Regime-agnostic by construction, so it earns in the tape where the directional profiles give profit back. Cointegration screen (correlation → regression → ADF), a new profile inside the confluence framework, shadow-first — never a bypass of the ≥70% gate.

Open

Open decisions & risks

  • ⚠Two F&O calibration rulings are under review. Weeklies stay excluded via SellRules.require_exact_expiry_future — flipping it takes the engine from 33/42 to 42/42 producing days, but a NIFTY weekly chain carries ~1% of the monthly's OI, so the fill model would overstate credit. Needs a chain-level liquidity gate first. Second ruling: stale vol gates warn rather than reject. Review evidence is automatic — read §7 of the daily report, don't reconstruct it.
  • ⚠Cross-day open risk is uncapped. 49.6% of capital at risk across 24 open positions; the circuit breaker is a daily-realised-loss and trades/day cap. Ranked #3 above.
  • ⚠Two EOD feeds lag one session. fo_bhavcopy and fii_dii_daily trail the last trading day, and they feed §7 F&O health plus the flow factor. The ≤21-day self-heal should absorb it — verify that it did rather than assuming, which is the mistake that let an earlier month-long outage hide.
  • ✕No new frontend dependency can be installed. A snap refresh pruned the pnpm store, so pnpm add fails with ERR_PNPM_UNEXPECTED_STORE until store-dir is repointed outside ~/snap/ and one full pnpm install runs. This is why virtualization is an in-repo hook rather than a library. It also means make check as a single target cannot run inside a git worktree — its ESLint leg shells out to pnpm install.
  • ◐Local main is 11 commits ahead of origin. Push is manual and yours; history on main is linear, so phase branches merge --ff-only.
  • ◐The 30-day paper clock is a Phase-7 gate, not a Phase-3 or Phase-6 one. It runs daily and was restarted 2026-07-03 by the sizing-bug fix. Phase 6 may well replace it with the honest version — N trades across ≥2 regimes, positive expectancy, bounded drawdown.
Guardrails

Invariants that bind everything above

  • ✕Live trading does not exist. The order path is paper-only — no Kite order, no GTT. Every “live” recommendation anywhere in the docs is a Phase-7 design constraint, not a description of something running.
  • ✕The engine is frozen. app/analysis/, app/backtest/engine.py and the window canon are bugfix-only, and the committed Rust fixtures are the parity oracle. Every finding on this page — regime gate, weight retune — is behaviour-changing and needs a §8 backtest regression plus explicit sign-off. Nothing here has changed the engine.
  • ◐Confluence only, ≥70% gate, no look-ahead. No signal from a single indicator; factors compute on candle N and are valid from N+1; committed signals come only from complete candles, and the tick-level layer is labelled provisional and never enters backtests or P&L.
  • ◐Reject, never clamp. A signal whose natural SL exceeds its class cap is rejected, not tightened. Sizing is mandatory on every signal, risk_pct is a whole percent, and paper entries size risk-first from the actual fill so a chased fill shrinks quantity instead of over-risking.
  • ✓The daily-loss circuit breaker is never disableable. Not weakened, not configurable-off, not for tests. Any new risk gate is added alongside it.
