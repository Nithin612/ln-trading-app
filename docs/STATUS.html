Gate experiment — which lever to pull

Read-only backtest testing the 6.2 verdict before any engine change. The question: raise the confidence gate, skip the transitional regime, or both? Skipping the transitional regime (keeping the 70 gate) wins — it nearly doubles captured R and triples per-trade expectancy while keeping far more trades than a gate-80 bump.
