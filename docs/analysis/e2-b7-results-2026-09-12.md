⇒ ⛔ **PARKED ITEM RESOLVED: the hold-period lever is not a lever.**

## ⛔ A NEW DEFECT FOUND EN ROUTE — the swing corpus does not exclude through-stop fills

`[code]` `positional_probe.py` rejects a fill whose open has already gapped past the stop (3
references), and the live path refuses it unconditionally (`eligibility.through_stop_reason`,
plus the paper broker's pre-fill rejection). ⛔ **`swing_dependence_probe.py` has no such check.**

⚠ **Bounded honestly before anyone acts on it:** the probe already counts harness defect #4
(booked `hit_sl` with a positive return) at **2 of 185 = 1.08%**, and the T=0 cohort is 85%
*losses*, so this is **not** the main driver of the T=0 result. But the swing corpus — the basis
of `probe-185`, the E3 cell and the σ_R ladder — **admits fills the live order path would refuse,
and the positional corpus does not.** The two are not measuring the same population.
