**The rebound is measured from the print that defines the signal, and most of it is that print.**
- **G = P1530 ÷ C_t − 1 on book slots: mean −63.9 bps** (median −56.1, 3,095 slots whose bases
  agree exactly). The book's last trade sits about 64 bps below its official (VWAP) close.
- **The executable version** — entering at the official close, which is what the old regime
  actually offered — **nets −63.7 bps** (NW t −33.1). Of the ~36 bps the book rebounds from its
  last print, all of it, and more, lies between the last print and the close: from the close,
  the book keeps falling.
- **Ending s one bar earlier (at P1525), so that s and R_on share no print, turns the net to
  −14.5 bps (NW t −9.0).** Removing the shared print removes the effect and then some. That is
  the signature of a bid-ask bounce on the last trade, not of a premium paid for immediacy.
- The CNC E1 (IC −0.249, t −54) is therefore not an estimate of tradeable information: an IC
  between a move ending at a print and a return starting at the same print is inflated by that
  print's noise by construction. The MIS E1, which shares no print, is +0.031 (t +9.8): a mild
  next-day *continuation*.

**The rest:**
- **Raw net +16.1 bps (t +5.5) vs middle-tercile-demeaned +0.03:** the difference is the

## 4. What NULL means — §9 of the pre-registration

- **PR-1 stops. Nothing is re-specified.** The executable and P1525 variants above are
  descriptives; promoting either to a decision would be a new trial on data already read.
- **PR-2 may still run under its own pre-registration (§9a, committed with the freeze), counted
  as a new trial.** Its build prerequisites 2, 3, 4 and 6 are not built. Its first read needs
  126 auction-era sessions (≈ April 2027 at the earliest).
- **How much a NULL here says about the auction regime (§10i).** The old close spread the forced
  flow over 30 minutes; the auction concentrates it in one executable print. So a PR-1 NULL is
  weaker evidence against an auction-era effect than against an old-regime one. The descriptives
  cut the other way, though: the old-regime "reversal" was, by these numbers, largely the last
  print's bounce, and the auction regime has no such last continuous print to bounce from.
