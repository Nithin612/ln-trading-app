## ⭐⭐ What this run actually shows

**1. Every trade the live engine would have refused was a same-session exit.** The delete
treatment removed **4 of 350 (1.14%)**, and the `T = 0` arm went **44 → 40** while `T ≥ 1`
stayed at **306**. All four sat in the same-session cohort. That is the gap-through-stop
archetype end to end: the fill opens through its own stop, the trade is closed that session,
and M64 books it at exactly **+1.000R**. Removing them pulled the T=0 mean DOWN, which is
the direction M64 predicts and the opposite of what a random deletion would do.

**The four, named, because they are M64 demonstrated on real data for the first time** —
until now it had only been shown on constructed gaps:

| symbol | dir | T | fill | stop | stop width | R booked |
|---|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| HFCL | BUY | 0 | 75.50 | **75.60** | 0.13% | **+1.0000** |
| MUTHOOTFIN | BUY | 0 | 3205.00 | **3236.00** | 0.97% | **+1.0000** |
| TCS | SELL | 0 | 3504.00 | **3480.00** | 0.68% | **+1.0000** |
| WIPRO | SELL | 0 | 412.55 | **410.20** | 0.57% | **+1.0000** |

Every fill is on the wrong side of its own stop (below it for the BUYs, above it for the
SELLs). Every one exits the same session. **Every one books exactly +1.0000R**, to four
decimal places, which is the whole of M64: the metric cannot see the defect because the
defect always produces the same number, and that number is indistinguishable from a genuine
winner. Mean R of the refused set is **+1.0000**.

⚠ And their stop widths — 0.13%, 0.57%, 0.68%, 0.97% — are all **below the 2% floor the
notional cap already enforces**, which is exactly where M85 predicted the contamination
lives and nowhere else.

⭐ **This is the first non-zero delete rate in item 6.** D5 and D1 returned 0 of 3,267 and
0 of 2,029 — their corpora are daily swing/positional signals on liquid names with a median
**5.000%** stop, and a 5% adverse overnight gap essentially does not happen. The probe's
corpus is different and, as the build queue already recorded, **`swing_dependence_probe` has
no through-stop exclusion while the positional probe and the live path do** — so this is
precisely the corpus where the two populations were never the same. Item 6 has now measured
the size of that difference: **1.14%**.

**2. The T=0 effect is NOT an artifact of impossible fills, and the standing caveat was
wrong about that.** The published note reads *"T=0 contrast t = −4.16 but it is the
tight-stop cohort the order path refuses"*. Refusing them removes 4 trades and the contrast
gets **STRONGER**: −0.6644 → **−0.8238**, t −4.73 → **−6.61** clustered. Whatever the
same-session cohort is, it survives being restricted to trades live would actually take.

**3. The dependence correction is small here.** ICC +0.086, design effect **1.09×**; the
contrast moves t −5.06 → −4.73 naive-to-clustered. Worth doing, not decisive — same story as
D5 and D1. The 6× inflation that killed RVOL's t = +3.67 needed a market-wide DAILY
regressor on 92 dates; 350 trades over 175 sessions is a far milder grouping.

**4. The excursion surface still says NO geometry repair.** MFE/|MAE| is **1.077** (1.017
delete-treated) — excursions are close to symmetric, not `MFE >> |MAE|`, and realised R is
−0.013 (−0.025 treated) at |t| ≤ 0.48. The published test was *"MFE much greater than |MAE|
with realised R < 0 would mean the entry HAS information and the barrier placement hands it
back"*. A ratio of ~1.0 does not meet that bar. **The conclusion is unchanged.**

## ⛔ The same reproducibility defect as D5/D1, and it applies here too

`swing_dependence_probe.load_frames` ranks the top 250 on `now() - interval '180 days'` —
the M62 look-ahead — and, like `_load_frames`, it RE-DERIVES its name set on every run
against state that has since changed. The published MFE/|MAE| of **0.83 / 1.12** was
measured on a set this run cannot rebuild; mine reads **1.249 / 1.160**. ⇒ **compare the
before/after columns within this run, never a number here against a published one.** The
fix is the same: pin the name list (`liquid_as_of`, item 14), do not re-derive it.
